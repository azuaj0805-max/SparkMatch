Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Meridian ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, age, city, and profile photos
  • Career information (job title, company, salary range)
  • Dating preferences and prompt answers
  • Work email address (for verification)
  • Messages sent through the app
  • Device information and push notification tokens

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match you with other users
  • To send push notifications about matches and messages
  • To verify your workplace via work email
  • To improve our services
  • To prevent fraud and ensure safety

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With other users as part of the matching experience (profile info, photos, prompts)
  • With service providers (Supabase for database, Expo for notifications)
  • When required by law

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure hosted in the United States. Photos are stored in secure cloud storage.

5. Your Rights

  • Access and update your profile at any time
  • Delete your account and all associated data from Settings
  • Opt out of push notifications in your device settings
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us

6. Age Requirement

Meridian is only available to users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Salary Information

Salary range information you provide is used only for matching purposes and is shown only to verified matches. We do not share or sell this information to third parties.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@meridianapp.co
