## Description (4000 chars max)
Meridian is the dating app built for ambitious professionals who are serious about who they spend their time with.

Unlike other dating apps, Meridian puts your career and ambition front and center — because what you're building matters just as much as who you are.

**What makes Meridian different:**

→ Career-first profiles — see job titles, companies, and salary ranges upfront
→ Salary verification — verified users get a badge so you know it's real
→ Thoughtful matching — like someone by reacting to their photos and prompts
→ Quality over quantity — 4 likes per day keeps things intentional
→ 5 match limit — so you actually focus on real conversations
→ Work email verification — confirm where you work with your company email

**How it works:**
1. Build your profile with 5 photos and 3 prompts
2. Browse ambitious professionals near you
3. React to their photos or prompts to like them
4. Match and start a real conversation

Meridian is for people who know what they want — in their career and in their relationships.
